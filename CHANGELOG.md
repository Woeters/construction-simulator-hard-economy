# Changelog

## 0.1.0-beta

- Initial public beta release.
- Increased machine rental prices to 10x their vanilla values.
- Added an upfront charge for the first 24 hours of every rental.
- Increased fuel and maintenance costs by 4x.
- Increased regular machine transport costs by 10x.
- Increased crane transport costs by 5x.
- Doubled construction-material and bulk-material purchase prices.
- Increased warehouse and direct construction-site delivery multipliers by 5x.
- Increased regular fast-travel costs by 10x.
- Added distance-based costs when traveling directly to a vehicle through the map or vehicle list.
- Reduced machine resale value from 75% to 50% of its purchase price.
- Made instant bulk filling substantially more expensive.
- Removed the money reward from instant emptying.
- Reduced physical bulk-material resale income.
- Scaled crane setup costs with the value of the crane.
- Added file validation, automatic backups, restore support and rejection of unsupported or modified files.
